Mist drapes low on the emerald crests,
As silver rain surrenders its hold,
A quiet chill fills the mountain air,
Wrapped in whispers of green and gold.

Pine trees stand like shadows in steam,
Over tea slopes silent and still,
While tea leaves glisten with heavy drops,
On the slopes of the sleepy hill.

Inside, a cup of warm Ceylon brews,
A fireplace crackles soft and low,
Outside, the eerie, fog-bound world—
Inside, a cozy, amber glow
